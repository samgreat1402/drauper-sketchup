# Multicolor CAD Icon Family — v4.1.34

## Semantic palette

- Existing geometry: dark blue-gray.
- Selected/target geometry: Draupr blue.
- Motion and operation direction: orange.
- Added/healed geometry: green.
- Cut, removed, or detached geometry: red.
- Glazing and transparent panels: translucent cyan.

## Acceptance

1. Verify all 17 Create and 18 Modify commands use at least one function-appropriate semantic layer.
2. Check icons at 20, 24, 27, and 32 px and at 100–200% display scaling.
3. Verify light/dark, hover, active, disabled, destructive, EN/FA, RTL, and keyboard-focus states.
4. Confirm colored overlays clarify the operation without depending on color alone.
5. Confirm every tooltip and accessible name still matches its command.
