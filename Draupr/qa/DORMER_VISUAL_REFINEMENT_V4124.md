# Dormer visual refinement — v4.1.24

## Screenshot review

The geometry and hosting were working, but the front windows dominated the
facade, the graphite frame material was also used for every exterior trim
piece, corner boards read as heavy black strips, and the window lacked a clear
sash/casing hierarchy.

## Refinement

- Rebalanced the window to approximately 56% of facade width and 62% of fitted
  wall height while retaining minimum-size protection.
- Reduced sash-member thickness.
- Added a proportional meeting rail and retained the optional center mullion.
- Added separate outer casing, apron, drip cap, and projecting sill.
- Added slimmer facade corner boards with short cheek returns.
- Added a dedicated **Trim and Fascia Material** control.
- Introduced the default `Draupr Trim - Warm White` finish.
- Separated `frame` and `trim` material roles for later editing.
- Applied trim material consistently to rake and eave fascia.
- Updated the outdated Dormer UI guidance.

## Native acceptance

Inspect every Dormer type at near, medium, and distant zoom. Check casing
clearance, window proportions, corner returns, fascia continuity, material
assignment, edit/rebuild, and a solid facade with Front Window disabled.
