# Dormer visual polish — v4.1.26

## Screenshot diagnosis

The hosted geometry was correct, but the three curved Dormer types displayed
every one of their twenty facet boundaries as a heavy black line. Their front
fascia repeated the same segmentation as visible white blocks. Window casing,
corner boards, drip caps, and fascia were also competing at nearly equal visual
weight.

## Refinement

- Hide, soften, and smooth matching internal facet edges on Eyebrow, Segmental,
  and Barrel roof shells.
- Apply the same seam treatment to curved front soffit and fascia segments.
- Preserve perimeter, ridge, eave, and host-intersection edges.
- Reduce casing, drip-cap, sill, corner-board, and fascia dimensions.
- Reduce fascia projection depth.
- Tone the default Warm White trim from brilliant white to a warmer neutral.
- Keep geometry and material roles independently editable.

## Native acceptance

Review curved types with Hidden Geometry both off and on. With it off, the roof
and fascia should read as continuous curved assemblies without striped facet
boundaries. With it on, the underlying twenty-facet construction should remain
inspectable. Confirm all straight-edged Dormer ridges and perimeter edges remain
visible.
